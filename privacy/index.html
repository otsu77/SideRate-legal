SideRate privacy policy

Effective September 28, 2026

SideRate reads visible text on pages you visit solely to identify price strings and add a converted estimate in your browser. This processing occurs locally in the extension.

Data SideRate uses

SideRate does not collect, sell, or transmit page text, shopping-cart contents, payment details, account credentials, or browsing history. It stores your selected target currency, dollar-symbol preference, enable or disable preference, onboarding completion, and optional site origins with their site-specific preferences using browser sync storage. It stores exchange-rate cache data locally in the browser. Browser sync may synchronize these preferences between browsers according to your browser account settings.

Exchange-rate provider

To obtain exchange rates, SideRate requests public rate data from Frankfurter. Those requests are not sent with page contents by SideRate.

Data sharing and deletion

SideRate does not share user data with third parties. You can remove SideRate’s stored preferences and cached rates by removing the extension or clearing its browser storage.

Changes to this statement

If SideRate’s data practices change, this statement will be updated before the change takes effect in a public release.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, contact siderate.app@gmail.com.

The extension requires host access to HTTP(S) pages because it must inspect visible page text to perform its core function. Browser-internal pages are excluded by the browser.

This statement will be updated before any future introduction of analytics, accounts, support tooling, or a server-side service.
